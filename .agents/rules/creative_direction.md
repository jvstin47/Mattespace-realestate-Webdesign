# Rule: Creative Direction — Architectural Short Film

The hero section is an interactive architectural short film where the visitor advances through carefully directed cinematic chapters.

## Golden Rule
> **"The visitor controls when the story advances. Mattespace controls how the story is experienced."**

## Architectural Directives
1. **Scroll as Continue Trigger**: A scroll gesture is a "continue" trigger, NEVER a transport scrubber.
2. **Pacing Control**: The app controls playback speed (native 60fps). The visitor only controls when the next scene begins.
3. **No Continuous Scrubbing**: Do not allow rapid flicks to bypass chapter playback.
4. **Visual Hierarchy**: Architecture is primary, typography is secondary, interface is tertiary.
